# Investigation

Start when: A core source, history or hypothesis question, including read-only requests.

1. Use [principle-pg-evidence-first](../../principle-pg-evidence-first/SKILL.md) and [research-postgresql](../../research-postgresql/SKILL.md) to establish the question, relevant paths, competing explanations and missing evidence.

2. Run only probes justified by the requested investigation. Keep implementation changes outside a read-only task.

3. Reconcile findings at the recorded revision and distinguish source, discussion, inference and unknowns.

Completion and output: Return a cited explanation, inspected locations, probe results and unresolved questions. Stop without editing source or drafting a submission unless separately requested.
